Project Summary

This rotating display was designed to easily display smaller decorative objects while being able to output high torque for heavier displayables. The design is a screwless enclosure that is held together by either physical indents or pressure between parts. A key design choice of the project was that it had to be 3D printable as that was the only available means of manufacturing.

Gearbox

A 2 stage planetary gearbox with a total 47:1 ratio was used in the design of the display. By using 2 7:1 gearboxes, the display was able to remain horizontally compact by increasing in verticality. The high ratio of gearbox was chosen not only to enable the display of heavier objects by amplifying the motor’s torque, but to additionally slow the rotation of the displayed object to ~49 seconds per revolution.

Fixture

The display comprises 5 main components, that being the flooring, walls, gearbox, motor and platform. Display flooring was designed to keep the motor in place with an indented ring to compensate for the motor extrusions on both sides while not needing a specific orientation when blindly attaching to the display’s walls. On the housing walls lie an extruded ring with a minor opening to align and secure the gearbox to the housing, additionally, a hole in the wall’s exterior was made to fit the wire which powers the main motor. The gearbox, as explained, is a 47:1 2 stage planetary gearbox designed to output higher torque for heavier displayable objects. Underneath the platform is an extruded ring which extends into and presses against the display's upper walls to keep the platform from tipping as objects are moved to the outer area of the platform. The motor powering the entire rotating display is a simple off-the-shelf motor from an Arduino starter kit.

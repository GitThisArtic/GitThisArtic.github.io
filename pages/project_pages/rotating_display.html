Project Summary

This rotating display was designed to easily display smaller decorative objects while being able to output high torque for heavier displayables. The period of rotation is ~47 seconds per revolution and is done by using 2 7:1 planetary gearboxes. Additionally, the display uses a screwless enclosure of 5 separate housing components, that being the flooring, walls, gearbox, motor and platform.

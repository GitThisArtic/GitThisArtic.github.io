Project Summary

Fall 2026 I joined UGA Robotics IEEE Hardware Competition team to assist in the development of an autonomous sensing robot. The robot must lap a racetrack 3 times while dodging obstacles and self parking in the pit every so often. The main goals of team and this project is balancing the cost to remain under $300 while still scoring as many game points as possible. Sensors on the robot are to include a camera for april tag reading; motor encoders, IMU and Odmeter for positioning, and ultrasonic sensors for object distance detection.
